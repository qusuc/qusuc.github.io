这是「职场英语场景」系列的第 7 篇，共 9 篇。每篇只讲一个真实场景，分三层：这个场景跑在哪些词上、可以照着念的句子、一段能直接丢给 AI 的角色扮演 prompt。

写给英语不是母语、却要用英语开完整个工作日的人。所有句子都是短句，能在会议中途扫一眼就念出来的那种。

**打字沟通有时间修改，所以标准更高。请求、催办、拒绝、报问题的模板，同一句话的三档语气，以及中式直译的常见陷阱。**

{{< we-panel >}}
**什么时候用**

- 在 Teams 上请人做事、催进度
- 要拒绝一个请求，但不想伤关系
- 往群里报一个问题或风险
- 写完不确定语气会不会太冲

**心态**

- 书面没有语气和表情兜底，所以中性写法读起来常常偏冷。多加一句缓冲，不加感叹号。
- 一条消息只讲一件事。要三件事就发三条，或者用编号。
- 先给结论再给背景。对方在手机上只看得到前两行。
- 发之前读一遍：如果这条是别人发给我的，我会不会不舒服？这一遍能拦住大部分问题。
{{< /we-panel >}}

## 一、术语

| English | 中文 | 用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **heads-up**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˈhɛdz ʌp/</span> | 提前知会 | 写法：Heads-up — the nightly run is red. 意思是先告诉你，不用你现在做什么。 |
| **to follow up on**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˈfɑloʊ ʌp/</span> | 跟进 | 催办的标准开头：Following up on the test account request. |
| **to circle back**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˈsɜrkəl bæk/</span> | 回头再说 | 用法：let me circle back on that tomorrow = 明天我再给你答复。 |
| **to loop in**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˈlup ɪn/</span> | 把某人拉进来 | 写法：looping in Sam, who owns the gateway. 比直接 @ 更礼貌。 |
| **FYI**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˌɛf waɪ ˈaɪ/</span> | 供你知晓 | 逐字念。表示不需要对方回复。滥用会显得冷，重要的事别用 FYI 开头。 |
| **at your convenience**<br><span class="we-ipa">/kənˈvinjəns/</span> | 你方便的时候 | 软化请求的好词，但真的不急才用。急事用它会让人误判优先级。 |
| **ASAP**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˌeɪ ɛs eɪ ˈpi/</span> | 尽快 | 慎用。它带压迫感，而且每个人理解的"尽快"不同。改成具体时间点。 |
| **gentle reminder**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˈdʒɛntl rɪˈmaɪndər/</span> | 温馨提醒 | 中国同事爱用，英语母语者会觉得有点被动攻击。直接说 following up 更自然。 |
| **no rush**<br><span class="we-ipa">/noʊ ˈrʌʃ/</span> | 不急 | 真不急才写。它能明显降低对方的压力。 |
| **to make sure I understand** | 我确认一下我的理解 | 书面确认的万能开头，读起来是负责，不是没看懂。 |
| **bandwidth**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˈbændwɪdθ/</span> | 精力/可用时间 | 拒绝时最好用的词：I don't have the bandwidth this week. |
| **to push back**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˈpʊʃ bæk/</span> | 提出异议、推回去 | 这是对上级说的词(I'd like to push back on that)，不是写给对方的。 |
| **to double-check**<br><span class="we-ipa">/ˈdʌbəl tʃɛk/</span> | 再确认一下 | — |
| **does that work for you** | 你看这样行吗 | 结尾问一句，把决定权交出去。几乎任何请求都能用它收尾。 |

## 二、场景句子

### Asking for something · 请人做事

<p class="we-groupnote">结构固定：一句背景、要什么、什么时候要、一句缓冲。四行以内。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Hi Sam — quick one. Could you create a test account on UAT for me? I need it to start the 5.17 regression.</span><span class="we-cn">Sam，一件小事。能帮我在 UAT 上建个测试账号吗？我要用它开始 5.17 的回归。</span><span class="we-tip">quick one 先给出体量，对方一看就知道不用花很久。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">If you can get to it by Wednesday that would unblock me. No rush before then.</span><span class="we-cn">周三之前弄好就能让我继续。在那之前不急。</span><span class="we-tip">给日期，并说明为什么是这个日期。比 ASAP 有效得多。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Could you take a look at this when you have a moment?</span><span class="we-cn">你有空的时候能看一下这个吗？</span><span class="we-tip">比 please help me to check 自然得多，后者是典型的中式写法。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Two things, and the second one can wait.</span><span class="we-cn">两件事，第二件不急。</span><span class="we-tip">同时提两件事时先标好优先级，对方不会两件都拖。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Does that work for you?</span><span class="we-cn">你看这样行吗？</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Chasing without nagging · 催办，但不催命

<p class="we-groupnote">催第一次给背景，第二次给影响，第三次升级并当面说要升级。不要连发三条一样的。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Following up on the test account — any luck?</span><span class="we-cn">跟进一下测试账号的事，有进展吗？</span><span class="we-tip">第一次催。any luck 很轻，不带责备。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Just checking whether this is still on your list. Happy to ask someone else if you're swamped.</span><span class="we-cn">确认一下这个还在你清单上吗。你要是忙不过来，我去问别人也行。</span><span class="we-tip">第二次催。给了台阶，同时暗示你会另找人。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">This one is now blocking the regression, so I need to sort it today. Can you or should I ask Sam?</span><span class="we-cn">这个现在挡住回归了，今天必须解决。你来还是我问 Sam?</span><span class="we-tip">第三次催。说清影响，给二选一。不带情绪。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Sorry to chase — is there an update on this?</span><span class="we-cn">抱歉催一下，这个有更新吗？</span><span class="we-tip">sorry to chase 是地道说法，比 gentle reminder 好。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">No reply needed if it's already done — I just can't see it from my side.</span><span class="we-cn">如果已经做好了就不用回，我这边看不到而已。</span><span class="we-tip">给对方一个不用回复的出口，催办就不会像质问。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Saying no in writing · 书面拒绝

<p class="we-groupnote">三段：承认对方的需要、说不能做什么、给一个能做的。缺最后一段就会显得冷。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I understand this is urgent for you. I don't have the bandwidth this week.</span><span class="we-cn">我理解这对你很急。这周我实在排不开。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">What I can do is cover the call path on Thursday. The rest would have to wait until next week.</span><span class="we-cn">我能做的是周四覆盖呼叫路径，其余的要等下周。</span><span class="we-tip">一定要给这一段。拒绝加替代方案，对方通常就接受了。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">If this has to happen this week, something else comes off my list. Which one should it be?</span><span class="we-cn">如果这周一定要做，我清单上得拿掉一件。你觉得该是哪件？</span><span class="we-tip">把优先级还给对方，这是最有力也最不伤和气的拒绝。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">That's outside what I can sign off on. Let's get Sam's view.</span><span class="we-cn">这超出我能拍板的范围了，我们听听 Sam 的意见。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Thanks for thinking of me on this one.</span><span class="we-cn">谢谢你想到我。</span><span class="we-tip">拒绝之后加一句，关系就不会受损。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Reporting a problem to a channel · 往群里报问题

<p class="we-groupnote">群消息的第一行决定有没有人读。先写结论和影响，再写细节。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Heads-up — the UAT environment has been down since about nine.</span><span class="we-cn">先说一声：UAT 环境从九点左右开始挂了。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Impact — the 5.17 regression is stopped. Nothing else is affected as far as I can see.</span><span class="we-cn">影响：5.17 的回归停了。据我看其他没受影响。</span><span class="we-tip">主动写影响面，包括没影响到什么。这能防止群里恐慌。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Looping in Sam, who owns the gateway.</span><span class="we-cn">把 Sam 拉进来，网关是他负责的。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'll update this thread when it's back.</span><span class="we-cn">恢复了我在这个 thread 里更新。</span><span class="we-tip">承诺更新，并且真的更新。这是群里建立信任最快的方式。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Update — back up as of 10:40. Root cause still unknown, I'll follow up tomorrow.</span><span class="we-cn">更新：10:40 恢复。根因还不清楚，我明天跟进。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Tone dials — the same message three ways · 语气三档：同一件事的三种写法

<p class="we-groupnote">同一个意思，软、中、硬。写完对照一下自己落在哪一档，再决定要不要调。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Whenever you get a chance, could you take a look at BUG-4821?</span><span class="we-cn">【软】你有空的时候能看一下 BUG-4821 吗？</span><span class="we-tip">用于不急、对方级别高、或者你在求人。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Could you look at BUG-4821 today? It's blocking the regression.</span><span class="we-cn">【中】今天能看一下 BUG-4821 吗？它挡住回归了。</span><span class="we-tip">默认档。绝大多数情况用这一档。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">BUG-4821 needs to be looked at today, or the release slips. Please let me know either way.</span><span class="we-cn">【硬】BUG-4821 今天必须有人看，否则版本要延。无论如何请回我一句。</span><span class="we-tip">只用在真的会有后果时。用多了就不灵了。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'd like to understand the reasoning here before we go ahead.</span><span class="we-cn">【硬，但克制】在往下走之前，我想先理解一下这么定的理由。</span><span class="we-tip">不同意但不想撕破脸时的写法。比 I disagree 好用。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Chinese-to-English traps · 中式直译陷阱

<p class="we-groupnote">这几条不是语法错，是读起来不对劲。母语者不会说，但也不会告诉你。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Could you take a look at this? — not "Please help me to check this."</span><span class="we-cn">【改】help me to check 是最典型的中式写法，母语者用 take a look。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Following up on this. — not "Gentle reminder."</span><span class="we-cn">【改】gentle reminder 在英语里读起来有点被动攻击，直接说在跟进更自然。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Could you get this to me by Thursday? — not "Please do it ASAP."</span><span class="we-cn">【改】ASAP 带压迫感且各人理解不同，换成具体日期。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I have a question about the spec. — not "I have a question want to ask you."</span><span class="we-cn">【改】want to ask you 是直译，而且语法不通。一句 I have a question about X 就够。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Thanks for your patience. — not "Thanks for your understanding" after every message.</span><span class="we-cn">【改】thanks for your understanding 只在你确实给对方添了麻烦时用，不是万能结尾。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Just so I understand — do you mean X or Y? — not "Please note that..."</span><span class="we-cn">【改】please note that 读起来像通知函，同事之间不用它。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Sorry, one more thing. — not "Sorry to bother you again and again."</span><span class="we-cn">【改】过度道歉会削弱你说的内容，而且英语里不这么堆。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Confirming and closing a thread · 确认与收口

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">To make sure I understand — you want the flag off for all users in week one, correct?</span><span class="we-cn">我确认一下理解：你希望第一周对所有用户关闭那个开关，对吗？</span><span class="we-tip">书面确认的标准写法。重要的事一定要有这一步，而且留下了记录。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Summarising what we agreed — 1) flag off in week one, 2) BUG-4821 in the hotfix, 3) review Monday.</span><span class="we-cn">总结一下我们的一致意见：1)第一周关开关，2)BUG-4821 进紧急修复，3)周一复盘。</span><span class="we-tip">编号写。会后发一条这个，等于把口头讨论钉死。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">If I've got any of that wrong, correct me here.</span><span class="we-cn">如果哪条我理解错了，在这里纠正我。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Thanks — that's clear now.</span><span class="we-cn">谢谢，现在清楚了。</span></div>

### Recovery — when you did not understand (written) · 没听懂的时候（书面版）

<p class="we-groupnote">书面反而更好办：你有时间读三遍，还能要求对方举例。看不懂就问，不要靠猜然后做错。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Sorry, I'm not sure I follow. Do you mean X, or Y?</span><span class="we-cn">抱歉我不太确定跟上了。你的意思是 X 还是 Y?</span><span class="we-tip">二选一。比单说 I don't understand 有效得多。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Could you give me an example? I want to make sure I test the right thing.</span><span class="we-cn">能给个例子吗？我想确保测的是对的东西。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Just to check I've read this right — you're saying the fix is already merged?</span><span class="we-cn">确认一下我读对了：你是说修复已经合进去了？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I looked it up but I'm still not sure — what does that acronym mean here?</span><span class="we-cn">我查了但还是不确定，那个缩写在这里指什么？</span><span class="we-tip">先说你查过了，再问。这样问缩写完全不尴尬。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Easier to talk than type — do you have five minutes for a call?</span><span class="we-cn">打字不如说话，你有五分钟能通个话吗？</span><span class="we-tip">来回三条还没说清就转语音。别在文字里耗。</span></div>

## 三、和 AI 对练

把下面这段整块复制给 ChatGPT、Claude 或任何一个会聊天的模型——代码块右上角有复制按钮，点一下就是整段。把方括号里的内容换成你自己的处境，然后用英文开口。

它会扮演对面那个人，不会放水：该反驳的反驳，该施压的施压。每一轮它都会附一行中文提示，告诉你对方刚用了什么招、什么答法能接住。你说结束之后，它会切出角色，用中文点评你哪里让步太早、漏了什么，并给出你本该说的两句英文。

```text {linenos=false}
We are messaging on Teams. I am the QA lead. You are playing my colleague on
the other end of the chat.

Your character: [Sam, a backend developer who owns the gateway. Friendly but
overloaded, slow to reply, and slightly defensive when chased.]
The situation: [I asked three days ago for a UAT test account. Nothing has
happened. It is now blocking my regression.]

How to play it:
- English only, written like real chat: short lines, lowercase sometimes, no
  formal letter style.
- Reply like a busy person. Sometimes answer only part of my message.
- React to my tone, not just my content. If I write something that lands as
  cold, blunt, or passive-aggressive, respond the way a real person would —
  go quiet, get defensive, or over-apologise — without explaining why.
- If I write something warm and clear, be helpful.
- Once, ask me a question whose answer is already in my earlier message, the
  way people do when they skim.
- Never break character to coach me mid-chat.

After each of your replies, add one line in Chinese, prefixed with 提示:,
saying how my last message read to you, and where it sat on the soft /
neutral / firm dial.

At the end, when I close the thread, drop character and review me in Chinese:
did I lead with the point, was the tone right for the situation, did I fall
into any Chinese-to-English traps, and rewrite my two weakest messages.

Wait for my first message.
```

### 示范对话

下面是这段 prompt 跑起来大概是什么样子。中文提示是给你看的，不是对话的一部分。

<div class="we-turn we-you"><span class="we-who">你</span><span class="we-en">Hi Sam — following up on the UAT test account. Any luck?</span><span class="we-cn">Sam，跟进一下 UAT 测试账号的事，有进展吗？</span><span class="we-hint">中性档，开头很好。注意没有用 gentle reminder。</span></div>

<div class="we-turn we-them"><span class="we-who">对方</span><span class="we-en">hey, sorry — been buried in the gateway migration. remind me what you needed?</span><span class="we-cn">嘿，抱歉，一直陷在网关迁移里。你要的是什么来着？</span><span class="we-hint">他在略读，信息其实三天前给过。不要不耐烦，重发一次并把影响加上。</span></div>

<div class="we-turn we-you"><span class="we-who">你</span><span class="we-en">No problem. A test account on UAT with call permissions — I need it to start the 5.17 regression. It's blocking me now.</span><span class="we-cn">没关系。UAT 上一个带通话权限的测试账号，我要用它开始 5.17 的回归。现在挡住我了。</span></div>

<div class="we-turn we-them"><span class="we-who">对方</span><span class="we-en">right. i can probably do it friday</span><span class="we-cn">明白。我大概周五能弄。</span><span class="we-hint">周五太晚了。推回去，但要给他一条出路，不要硬顶。</span></div>

<div class="we-turn we-you"><span class="we-who">你</span><span class="we-en">Friday would put the regression a week behind. Can you or should I ask someone on your team?</span><span class="we-cn">周五的话回归要落后一周。你来还是我问你们组其他人？</span></div>

<div class="we-turn we-them"><span class="we-who">对方</span><span class="we-en">no need to escalate, i'll see what i can do</span><span class="we-cn">不用升级，我看看能怎么办。</span><span class="we-hint">他把你的二选一读成了施压。这里加一句缓冲，把关系拉回来。</span></div>

<div class="we-turn we-you"><span class="we-who">你</span><span class="we-en">Not escalating — happy to take it off your plate if you're swamped. Whatever's easier for you.</span><span class="we-cn">不是要升级，你要是忙不过来我可以自己去弄。哪种对你方便都行。</span></div>

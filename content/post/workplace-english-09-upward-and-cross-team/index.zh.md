这是「职场英语场景」系列的第 9 篇，共 9 篇。每篇只讲一个真实场景，分三层：这个场景跑在哪些词上、可以照着念的句子、一段能直接丢给 AI 的角色扮演 prompt。

写给英语不是母语、却要用英语开完整个工作日的人。所有句子都是短句，能在会议中途扫一眼就念出来的那种。

**向上汇报、要人要时间、升级问题、拒绝不合理的请求，以及跟你的上级说不同意。**

{{< we-panel >}}
**什么时候用**

- 跟上级汇报或要资源
- 一个问题在同级解决不了，要升级
- 别的组要你临时接一批活
- 你不同意上级的决定

**心态**

- 对上汇报第一句永远是结论，不是过程。你的上级时间比你少，注意力比你短。
- 要资源要说清代价：不是"我很忙"，是"这周多这件事，就少那件事，你选"。
- 升级不是告状。当着当事人的面说你要升级，把双方立场都带上去。
- 不同意上级时，分清是判断分歧还是信息差。先补信息，再谈判断。
{{< /we-panel >}}

## 二、场景句子

### Reporting up — headline first · 对上汇报：先给结论

<p class="we-groupnote">上级只需要三样：结论、风险、你要什么。过程他不关心，除非他问。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Two minutes — one good thing, one risk, one ask.</span><span class="we-cn">两分钟：一个好消息、一个风险、一个请求。</span><span class="we-tip">开场就给结构。上级会立刻知道要听多久、听什么。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">The headline is — 5.17 is on track, and the crash rate is the one thing I'm watching.</span><span class="we-cn">结论是：5.17 进度正常，崩溃率是我唯一在盯的东西。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I don't need anything from you on this one. Just keeping you informed.</span><span class="we-cn">这件事不需要你做什么，只是让你知道。</span><span class="we-tip">主动说清不需要行动，你的上级会更愿意听你说话。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">If you only remember one thing from this — we're one person short for the 5.18 cycle.</span><span class="we-cn">如果只记住一件事：5.18 周期我们缺一个人。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Do you want the detail, or is that enough?</span><span class="we-cn">你要细节吗？还是这样就够了？</span><span class="we-tip">把长度的决定权交出去，你就不会讲太多或太少。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Asking for resources · 要人、要时间

<p class="we-groupnote">不要说你很忙。说清楚多这件事就少哪件事，让上级做选择，而不是让他同情你。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'm not asking to move the date. I'm asking for two people for two days.</span><span class="we-cn">我不是要改日期，我是要两个人两天。</span><span class="we-tip">先说不要什么。这一句能立刻降低对方的防御。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Here's the trade-off — with the team we have, I can cover the call path or the group calls, not both.</span><span class="we-cn">这是取舍：以现在的人力，我能覆盖呼叫路径或者群通话，不能两个都要。</span><span class="we-tip">二选一的取舍是最有力的要资源方式。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Help me prioritise. If both have to happen, which one do you want first?</span><span class="we-cn">帮我定优先级。如果两件都要做，你希望哪件先？</span><span class="we-tip">把选择还给上级，同时也是在保护你自己。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">The team is at capacity. Anything new displaces something existing.</span><span class="we-cn">团队已经满负荷。加新的就要挤掉旧的。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'd rather tell you now than tell you in three weeks that we're late.</span><span class="we-cn">我宁可现在告诉你，也不想三周后告诉你我们晚了。</span><span class="we-tip">说明你为什么现在就提。这会让"提前预警"变成你的优点。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Escalating · 升级一个问题

<p class="we-groupnote">顺序：先当面告诉当事人你要升级，再上去，而且把对方的立场也带上去。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Before I escalate this, is there anything else we can try today?</span><span class="we-cn">在我升级之前，今天还有别的能试的吗？</span><span class="we-tip">对当事人说的。给最后一次机会，而且是当面说的。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I want to bring something to you that Sam and I couldn't settle.</span><span class="we-cn">有件事我和 Sam 没能达成一致，想带给你。</span><span class="we-tip">对上级说的开头。用"没能达成一致"，不用"他不配合"。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Here's my position, and here's his — I think both are reasonable.</span><span class="we-cn">这是我的立场，这是他的，我认为两边都有道理。</span><span class="we-tip">带上对方的立场。这一句让升级不像告状，而且让你显得可信。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'm not asking you to decide who's right. I'm asking for a decision so we can move.</span><span class="we-cn">我不是要你判谁对，我是要一个决定好让我们继续。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">This has been open for a week, and it's now on the critical path.</span><span class="we-cn">这个开了一周了，现在在关键路径上。</span><span class="we-tip">说清楚为什么现在升级，而不是上周或下周。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Saying no to another team · 拒绝别的组

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I want to help, and I need to be honest about what that would cost.</span><span class="we-cn">我想帮忙，也需要如实说清代价。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">We could take it, but the 5.17 regression would slip by three days. Is that acceptable to you?</span><span class="we-cn">我们可以接，但 5.17 的回归会延三天。你能接受吗？</span><span class="we-tip">把代价放在对方面前，大部分请求会自己消失。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">That's not realistic with the team we have this month.</span><span class="we-cn">以我们这个月的人力，这不现实。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">The right person for this is the platform team. Want me to introduce you?</span><span class="we-cn">这件事更适合平台组。要我帮你引荐吗？</span><span class="we-tip">拒绝的同时给一条路，关系就不会变差。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">If your lead and mine agree it's the priority, I'll make room.</span><span class="we-cn">如果你和我的上级都同意这是优先项，我来腾时间。</span><span class="we-tip">不是拒绝，是把决定推到该做决定的层级。非常好用。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Disagreeing with your manager · 跟上级说不同意

<p class="we-groupnote">先分清是信息差还是判断分歧。信息差就补信息，判断分歧就说清楚你会执行。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Can I give you a different view before this is final?</span><span class="we-cn">在定下来之前，我能给一个不同的看法吗？</span><span class="we-tip">问一句，而不是直接反驳。这一句常常就把门打开了。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'd like to push back on the date, and I'll explain why.</span><span class="we-cn">我想对这个日期提出异议，我说说原因。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">You may know something I don't — is there a commitment behind this date?</span><span class="we-cn">你可能知道我不知道的事：这个日期背后有什么承诺吗？</span><span class="we-tip">先假设是信息差。很多时候确实是，而且这个问法保住了双方的面子。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">My concern is that we'd be testing on the weekend, and quality goes down when we do.</span><span class="we-cn">我担心的是我们要周末测，而周末测质量会下降。</span><span class="we-tip">讲具体后果，不讲感受。不要说 I'm not comfortable 而不给原因。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Understood. I don't agree, but I'll make it work and I'll tell you early if it slips.</span><span class="we-cn">明白。我不同意，但我会把它做成，如果要延我会尽早告诉你。</span><span class="we-tip">分歧但服从的标准说法。说清楚立场，也说清楚你会执行。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Delivering bad news upward · 向上报坏消息

<p class="we-groupnote">越早越好，越短越好。三段：发生了什么、影响、你已经在做什么。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I have some bad news and a plan. Bad news first.</span><span class="we-cn">我有个坏消息和一个方案，先说坏消息。</span><span class="we-tip">先声明有方案，对方听坏消息时的反应会完全不同。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">We missed a regression in 5.16 that reached production. About eight percent of Android users are affected.</span><span class="we-cn">我们在 5.16 漏了一个回归问题，进了生产。大约 8% 的安卓用户受影响。</span><span class="we-tip">直接说事实和影响面。不要铺垫，不要先解释原因。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Here's what I've already done, and here's what I need from you.</span><span class="we-cn">这是我已经做的，这是我需要你做的。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'll write up what we missed and why, and bring it to you on Friday.</span><span class="we-cn">我会把漏掉了什么、为什么写下来，周五拿给你。</span><span class="we-tip">主动认领复盘。这一句能把一次事故变成你的加分项。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">That's on my team, and I own it.</span><span class="we-cn">这是我团队的问题，我来担。</span><span class="we-tip">该担的时候一句话担掉。推卸的代价比事故本身大得多。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Recovery — when you did not understand · 没听懂的时候

<p class="we-groupnote">跟上级或别组开会时，听不懂的成本是你答应了不该答应的事。宁可当场慢一点。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Before I answer — can I make sure I understood the question?</span><span class="we-cn">在我回答之前，我能先确认一下问题吗？</span><span class="we-tip">把追问放在回答之前，显得是谨慎，不是没跟上。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">You're asking whether we can do both, or which one comes first?</span><span class="we-cn">你问的是我们能不能两个都做，还是哪个先做？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I want to be careful before I commit to that. Can I confirm and come back to you today?</span><span class="we-cn">我想谨慎一点再承诺。我确认一下今天给你答复，行吗？</span><span class="we-tip">最有用的一句。永远不要在没听懂的情况下当场答应。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Can you send me that in writing? I want to get the details right.</span><span class="we-cn">能把那个书面发我吗？我想把细节弄准确。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Sorry — could you say that once more, a bit slower?</span><span class="we-cn">抱歉，能再说一遍吗，慢一点。</span></div>

## 三、和 AI 对练

把下面这段整块复制给 ChatGPT、Claude 或任何一个会聊天的模型——代码块右上角有复制按钮，点一下就是整段。把方括号里的内容换成你自己的处境，然后用英文开口。

它会扮演对面那个人，不会放水：该反驳的反驳，该施压的施压。每一轮它都会附一行中文提示，告诉你对方刚用了什么招、什么答法能接住。你说结束之后，它会切出角色，用中文点评你哪里让步太早、漏了什么，并给出你本该说的两句英文。

```text {linenos=false}
You are playing my manager, the head of engineering. I am the QA lead. We are
in a short one-to-one and I need something from you.

The situation: [my team is one person short for the 5.18 cycle. Another team
has just asked us to take on the API regression for their service as well. I
want either a second pair of hands or a decision about what comes off my
list.]

How to play it:
- English only. You are busy, direct, and you think in trade-offs. You do not
  hand out people easily.
- Make me be specific. If I say I am busy, ask what exactly would not get
  done. If I ask for two people, ask for how long and what they would do.
- Push the problem back to me at least once ("can't you just prioritise?").
- Have a piece of information I do not have — a commitment made above us that
  explains the date — and reveal it only if I ask a good question about why
  the date is what it is.
- At some point, agree to something quickly and vaguely, and see whether I
  pin down what was agreed before the meeting ends.
- If I ask you to repeat, slow down, or put something in writing, do it
  without commenting on my English.
- Never break character to coach me.

After each of your turns, add one line in Chinese, prefixed with 提示:,
saying what I just gave away or what a sharper ask would sound like.

At the end, drop character and review me in Chinese: did I lead with the
headline, did I present a trade-off rather than a complaint, did I leave with
something concrete, and two English sentences I should have used.

Wait for me to start.
```

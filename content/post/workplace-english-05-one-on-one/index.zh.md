这是「职场英语场景」系列的第 5 篇，共 9 篇。每篇只讲一个真实场景，分三层：这个场景跑在哪些词上、可以照着念的句子、一段能直接丢给 AI 的角色扮演 prompt。

写给英语不是母语、却要用英语开完整个工作日的人。所有句子都是短句，能在会议中途扫一眼就念出来的那种。

**定期 1:1 从开场到收尾的整套英文说法，包含表扬、提问题、谈发展、留人和告别这些难开口的部分。**

{{< we-panel >}}
**什么时候用**

- 每周或每两周固定的 1:1
- 有人状态明显不对，临时约一次
- 绩效周期前后要对齐期望
- 有人提出想换组或想离职

**心态**

- 你是主持人，节奏由你定。对方说得多才是好的 1:1，目标是让他讲 70% 的话。
- 一开始就说清楚英语不是你的母语，请对方慢一点。这会让整场对话都放慢，对你有利。
- 听不懂就直接说，不要点头蒙混。1:1 里蒙混的代价是你漏掉了他真正想说的事。
- 表扬要具体到某件事，批评要对事不对人。用 SBI：情境 → 行为 → 影响。
{{< /we-panel >}}

## 二、场景句子

### Opening — settle them in · 开场：让人放松下来

<p class="we-groupnote">前两分钟不要谈工作。开场固定说一遍英语声明，长期下来对方会习惯你的节奏。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Thanks for making the time. This is your meeting, not mine.</span><span class="we-cn">谢谢你抽时间。这是你的会，不是我的。</span><span class="we-tip">一句话定调，比任何寒暄都有用。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Before we start — my English is not my first language, so please speak slowly, and stop me if I look lost.</span><span class="we-cn">开始前说明一下，英语不是我的母语，请说慢一点，看我没跟上就打断我。</span><span class="we-tip">每次都说。这不丢人，而且真的有效。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">How have the last two weeks been for you?</span><span class="we-cn">过去两周你过得怎么样？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">How are you doing — not the work, you.</span><span class="we-cn">你自己怎么样？我问的不是工作，是你。</span><span class="we-tip">需要谈状态时用，对方通常会愣一下然后说真话。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Anything on your mind before I go through my list?</span><span class="we-cn">我说我的清单之前，你有什么想说的吗？</span><span class="we-tip">永远让对方先说，你的议程放后面。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Getting the real status · 问出真实状态，而不像审问

<p class="we-groupnote">少用 "why"，多用 "what" 和 "walk me through"。"Why did you..." 听起来像追责。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Walk me through what you worked on this week.</span><span class="we-cn">说说你这周做了什么。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">What's taking longer than you expected?</span><span class="we-cn">有什么比你预想的更花时间？</span><span class="we-tip">比 "are you behind schedule" 温和得多，但拿到的是同样的信息。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">What's blocking you right now?</span><span class="we-cn">现在什么卡着你？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Is there anything you are stuck on but haven't raised yet?</span><span class="we-cn">有没有什么你卡住了但还没提出来的？</span><span class="we-tip">这句常能挖出真问题。人不主动报坏消息。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">On a scale of one to five, how confident are you about that date?</span><span class="we-cn">一到五分，你对那个时间点有多大把握？</span><span class="we-tip">逼出真实判断的好办法，比问 "can you make it" 有效。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">What do you need from me that you're not getting?</span><span class="we-cn">有什么是你需要我给、但我没给的？</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Blockers and support · 阻塞与支持

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Let me take that one. I'll talk to the backend team.</span><span class="we-cn">这件事我来。我去跟后端聊。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I can't remove that, but I can buy you time. Let's push the date by two days.</span><span class="we-cn">这个我拿不掉，但我可以给你争取时间。我们把日期推两天。</span><span class="we-tip">做不到的事直说做不到，再给能做到的。这比空承诺更能建立信任。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Do you have the bandwidth for this, honestly?</span><span class="we-cn">说实话，你还忙得过来吗？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">If something has to slip, what should it be?</span><span class="we-cn">如果必须有东西往后放，你觉得应该是哪个？</span><span class="we-tip">把优先级的判断交给他，你只做确认。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Let's park that and come back to it next time.</span><span class="we-cn">这个先放一放，下次再谈。</span><span class="we-tip">park = 暂时搁置，不是取消。开会常用。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Positive feedback that lands · 表扬：要具体

<p class="we-groupnote">"Good job" 等于没说。用 SBI 的正面版本：哪件事 + 你做了什么 + 带来什么结果。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I want to call out something specific. The way you handled the crash report last Tuesday was exactly right.</span><span class="we-cn">我想具体说一件事。上周二你处理那个崩溃报告的方式完全对。</span><span class="we-tip">call out = 特别指出（正面）。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">You found that before it reached production. That saved us a hotfix.</span><span class="we-cn">你在它进生产之前发现了。这省了我们一个 hotfix。</span><span class="we-tip">说清楚影响，表扬才有重量。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I've noticed you've been picking things up without being asked. That's what I look for.</span><span class="we-cn">我注意到你主动接活，不用别人安排。这正是我看重的。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I told my manager about it.</span><span class="we-cn">这事我跟我的上级说了。</span><span class="we-tip">一句话，分量很重。做过就说，没做过别说。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Raising a problem — SBI · 提问题：情境-行为-影响

<p class="we-groupnote">顺序固定：先说哪件事(Situation)，再说他做了什么(Behaviour)，最后说造成什么(Impact)。全程不评价人。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'd like to give you some feedback. Is now a good time?</span><span class="we-cn">我想给你一些反馈，现在方便吗？</span><span class="we-tip">先问一句，对方就有心理准备，防御会低很多。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">In yesterday's release call, the test report wasn't ready when we started.</span><span class="we-cn">昨天的发版会上，我们开始的时候测试报告还没好。</span><span class="we-tip">Situation — 只讲事实，带时间地点。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">The result was that we spent twenty minutes waiting, and the decision moved to today.</span><span class="we-cn">结果是我们等了二十分钟，决定推到了今天。</span><span class="we-tip">Impact — 说影响，不说 "你让大家很被动"。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Help me understand what happened there.</span><span class="we-cn">帮我理解一下当时是什么情况。</span><span class="we-tip">比 "why didn't you..." 好一百倍。给他解释的空间。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">What I need going forward is the report in the channel the evening before.</span><span class="we-cn">往后我需要的是：前一天晚上把报告发到群里。</span><span class="we-tip">要求要具体到可执行，不要说 "be better prepared"。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">This is fixable, and I'm not worried about it. I just need it not to repeat.</span><span class="we-cn">这是能改的，我也不担心。我只是需要它不要再发生。</span><span class="we-tip">收尾降温。批评之后一定要给一个正面的关门句。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Career and expectations · 职业发展与期望对齐

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Where do you want to be in two years? Don't give me the safe answer.</span><span class="we-cn">两年后你想在什么位置？别给我标准答案。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">What part of the work do you actually enjoy?</span><span class="we-cn">这份工作里你真正喜欢的是哪部分？</span><span class="we-tip">排任务前问这个，比问 "你想做什么" 得到的答案真实。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">To move to the next level, I need to see you owning something end to end.</span><span class="we-cn">要上一个级别，我需要看到你从头到尾主导一件事。</span><span class="we-tip">讲晋升要给条件，不要给承诺。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I can't promise a promotion this cycle. What I can do is make sure the work is visible.</span><span class="we-cn">这个周期我不能承诺晋升。我能做的是让你的工作被看见。</span><span class="we-tip">诚实，而且给了实在的东西。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'd like to give you a stretch assignment. It will be uncomfortable. Are you up for it?</span><span class="we-cn">我想给你一个有挑战的任务。会不舒服。你愿意吗？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Let's be clear about expectations for the next quarter.</span><span class="we-cn">我们把下个季度的期望说清楚。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### When they bring bad news · 对方说了坏消息：想走、不开心、扛不住

<p class="we-groupnote">第一反应永远是问，不是劝。先把原因听完整，再决定要不要挽。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Thank you for telling me. I'd rather hear it now than in a resignation letter.</span><span class="we-cn">谢谢你告诉我。我宁可现在听到，也不想在辞职信上看到。</span><span class="we-tip">对方说不开心时的第一句。它决定了他后面愿不愿意说实话。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Help me understand — is it the work, the team, or the money?</span><span class="we-cn">帮我理解一下，是工作本身、团队，还是钱？</span><span class="we-tip">三选一的问法能快速定位，对方也容易回答。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">If nothing changes, how long do you see yourself staying?</span><span class="we-cn">如果什么都不变，你觉得自己还会待多久？</span><span class="we-tip">问得直接，但这是你最需要知道的数字。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">What would have to change for you to want to stay?</span><span class="we-cn">要发生什么变化，你才会想留下来？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I need a few days to see what I can do. Can we talk again on Friday?</span><span class="we-cn">我需要几天看看能做什么。我们周五再谈一次好吗？</span><span class="we-tip">不要当场承诺任何你说了不算的事。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I understand, and I respect the decision. Let's make the handover clean.</span><span class="we-cn">我理解，也尊重这个决定。我们把交接做干净。</span><span class="we-tip">留不住的时候说这句。体面收场，人会记很久。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Closing and commitments · 收尾与承诺

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Let me repeat back what I heard, to make sure I got it right.</span><span class="we-cn">我把我听到的复述一遍，确认我理解对了。</span><span class="we-tip">一箭双雕：既确认信息，又给你自己一次消化英语的机会。强烈建议每次都用。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">So — two things for me, one thing for you. Is that right?</span><span class="we-cn">所以，两件事在我这，一件在你那。对吗？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">I'll follow up on that by Wednesday.</span><span class="we-cn">这件事我周三前给你回音。</span><span class="we-tip">给具体日期。说了就要做到，1:1 的信任是这样攒的。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Are we on the same page?</span><span class="we-cn">我们对齐了吗？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Same time in two weeks. Bring anything that's bothering you.</span><span class="we-cn">两周后同一时间。有什么不舒服的都可以带过来。</span></div>

<hr class="we-sep">

### Recovery — when you did not understand · 没听懂的时候

<p class="we-groupnote">这一组最重要。听不懂是常态，装懂才是事故。选一句背熟，张口就来。</p>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Sorry, could you say that again?</span><span class="we-cn">抱歉，能再说一遍吗？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Could you slow down a little? I want to make sure I follow.</span><span class="we-cn">能说慢一点吗？我想确保跟上了。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Let me make sure I understand — you mean the test data was already stale, right?</span><span class="we-cn">我确认一下，你的意思是测试数据本来就是旧的，对吗？</span><span class="we-tip">复述确认。听懂了一半时用它补另一半，对方会自然把话展开。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">What do you mean by that exactly?</span><span class="we-cn">你说的具体是什么意思？</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Can you put that in the chat for me?</span><span class="we-cn">你能把这句发到聊天里吗？</span><span class="we-tip">术语或名字听不清时的杀手锏。写下来你就懂了。</span></div>

<div class="we-say"><span class="we-en">Give me a second to think about that.</span><span class="we-cn">让我想一下。</span><span class="we-tip">争取思考时间，不要用沉默硬扛。</span></div>

## 三、和 AI 对练

把下面这段整块复制给 ChatGPT、Claude 或任何一个会聊天的模型——代码块右上角有复制按钮，点一下就是整段。把方括号里的内容换成你自己的处境，然后用英文开口。

它会扮演对面那个人，不会放水：该反驳的反驳，该施压的施压。每一轮它都会附一行中文提示，告诉你对方刚用了什么招、什么答法能接住。你说结束之后，它会切出角色，用中文点评你哪里让步太早、漏了什么，并给出你本该说的两句英文。

```text {linenos=false}
You are playing a QA engineer on my team. I am your manager, and we are in
our regular one-to-one.

Your character: [name], [1-2 years / 5 years] on the team, works on
[the calling app's regression / API automation / the AI test platform].
You are [competent but quiet / strong but frustrated / new and still ramping up].
Right now you are privately unhappy about [being given only repetitive
regression work / a colleague getting the interesting project / unclear
promotion criteria], but you will not volunteer this in the first few turns.

How to play it:
- English only. Speak like a real engineer, not a textbook: short sentences,
  some hesitation, occasional tangents.
- Do not dump everything at once. Give a safe answer first. Open up only if I
  ask a real follow-up question or make you feel safe.
- If I ask a lazy question ("everything fine?"), give a lazy answer ("yeah,
  all good"). Reward good questions, not bad ones.
- Sometimes use a QA or VoIP term I may not know, and let me ask about it.
- If I say I did not understand, or ask you to repeat or slow down, do it
  naturally and without commenting on my English.
- Stay in character. Never break out to give me advice mid-conversation.

After each of your replies, add one line in Chinese, prefixed with 提示:,
saying what I could have asked instead, or what you are holding back.

At the end, when I close the meeting, drop character and give me a short
review in Chinese: what I did well, what I missed, and two English sentences
I should have used.

Start by waiting for me to open the meeting.
```

### 示范对话

下面是这段 prompt 跑起来大概是什么样子。中文提示是给你看的，不是对话的一部分。

<div class="we-turn we-you"><span class="we-who">你</span><span class="we-en">Thanks for making the time. Before we start — my English is not my first language, so please speak slowly. How have the last two weeks been?</span><span class="we-cn">谢谢你抽时间。开始前说明一下，英语不是我的母语，请慢一点。过去两周怎么样？</span></div>

<div class="we-turn we-them"><span class="we-who">对方</span><span class="we-en">Yeah, fine. Busy. I finished the regression round for 5.17 and picked up a couple of bugs from the crash report.</span><span class="we-cn">还行，挺忙。我做完了 5.17 的回归，又从崩溃报告里接了两个 bug。</span><span class="we-hint">"fine, busy" 是安全答案，不是真实答案。别接受它，追一句。</span></div>

<div class="we-turn we-them"><span class="we-who">对方</span><span class="we-en">Honestly, the regression. It's the third round this month and it's the same suite every time.
